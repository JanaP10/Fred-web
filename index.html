About

Meet Fred

Hi, I'm Fred — a bilingual French and English teacher from Québec with more than 10 years of teaching experience in Canada and abroad.

Over the years, I've worked with learners of different ages, backgrounds and learning styles, teaching both French and English as second languages. My international teaching experience has also taken me to Thailand and Saudi Arabia, giving me the opportunity to work with students from diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds.

My approach is patient, flexible and personalized. Whether you're learning French or English for everyday life, work, personal goals, or preparing for the TEF Canada exam, we'll focus on what you need to communicate with greater confidence.

My educational background includes English as a Second Language teaching, psychology, languages and pedagogy.
